Who we are

More than a branding
company — a family.

AREND GESIN is a family-based business that focuses on THAT Child and is youth-friendly. Comprising a highly skilled human resource base with broad professional experience in IT and project management, we commit to best-practice solutions in every area of our operation.

We believe in data as the new oil. We take empowerment very seriously and consider knowledge and skills transfer as the key to an empowered individual and organisation. As the world comes to terms with the pressures of the knowledge economy, we equip the youth and women with requisite skills in entrepreneurship, customer service, branding, and personal development.

With such competencies, they will be ready to live the dream of Kenya's Vision 2030 and the Sustainable Development Goals.

Our journey

How AGI came
to be.

✦

2020

AGI is founded in Nakuru

Arend Gesin Investment is established with core business in branding & imaging, driven by family values and a passion for youth empowerment.

✦

2020 – 2021

Expanding our service lines

We added stationery supplies, IT installations, and work safety equipment (PPEs) to better serve our growing client base across counties.

✦

2022

MIND MASTERS programme launched

We launched our flagship CSR initiative — inspiring, motivating and coaching youth in schools and institutions to unlock the power of their minds.

✦

2023 – 2024

Growing across 5 counties

Operations expanded across Nakuru, Nairobi, Uasin Gishu, Trans Nzoia and Bungoma — building a network of youth ambassadors and trusted partners.
