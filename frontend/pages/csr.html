The Heart of AGI

Who Is THAT Child?

"THAT Child is not a statistic. THAT Child is a name, a face, a future waiting to be unlocked."

🧒 The overlooked youth
🌍 In every community we serve
💛 Deserving better than today

When we say THAT Child, we mean the young person sitting at the back of the classroom who hasn't been told they matter. The teenager in our community who has never heard the words "you can do this." The youth who has talent, energy, and potential — but no platform, no mentor, and no path forward.

AGI was founded on the conviction that business should serve community. Every brand we build, every project we deliver, every client we serve — it all feeds back into our ability to show up for THAT Child. Profit with purpose is not a tagline for us. It is our operating model.

We focus on equipping young people and women with the skills they need — in entrepreneurship, branding, customer service, and personal development — so they can stand on their own, contribute to Kenya's Vision 2030, and build a better world.

"We believe data is the new oil — and that knowledge and skills transfer is the key to an empowered individual and organisation."

Three deliberate ways AGI turns business success into community impact.

🧠

MIND MASTERS Programme

Our flagship youth empowerment initiative that inspires confidence, motivates action, and coaches young people on the power of their minds. Delivered in learning institutions across the counties we serve.

🤝

CSR & Community Partnerships

We partner with other players and organisations within the sector to run CSR initiatives that go beyond charity — focusing on sustainable, skills-based empowerment for youth and families in our communities.

Our business model deliberately expands operations to youth — training them as brand ambassadors and entrepreneurial agents, creating an environment of genuine self-reliance and economic independence.

AGI Flagship Programme

The MIND MASTERS
Programme

A transformational experience designed to awaken the power within every young person — giving them the tools, mindset, and plan to live a life of purpose and impact.

01 ✨

Inspire

We create genuine confidence — helping young people see themselves clearly, recognise their worth, and believe deeply in their ability to change their circumstances.

02 🔥

Motivate

We create a feeling of "wanting to" — igniting the internal drive that no external force can manufacture. Motivated youth don't wait for opportunity. They create it.

03 🎯

Coach

We give practical skills — structured coaching on goal-setting, decision-making, personal development auditing, and building a concrete action plan for life.

In MIND MASTERS, participants learn to shape their own destiny:
✓
Set meaningful goals and make real decisions towards achieving them
✓
Understand and harness the power of the human mind
✓
Carry out a sincere Personal Development Audit (PDA)
✓
Build entrepreneurial thinking and customer service skills
✓
Develop a detailed, actionable Life Action Plan
✓
Align personal ambitions with Kenya Vision 2030 and the SDGs
Designed for individuals · learning institutions · corporates — with a primary focus on youth in schools and colleges across Kenya.
