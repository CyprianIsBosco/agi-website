Our Story

Where It All Began

"A family business built on the belief that every child deserves a chance — and that great branding can change a community."

🏠 Home of Branding

Arend Gesin Investment (AGI) was established in 2020 as a family-centred business with a clear purpose: to deliver world-class branding services while deliberately investing in the youth and communities we operate in.

At the heart of everything we do is THAT Child — the young person in our community who deserves better opportunities, better skills, and a brighter future. We believe that business can be both profitable and purposeful.

Comprising a highly skilled team with broad professional experience in IT, branding, and project management, we bring a rare combination of technical expertise and genuine human warmth to every client relationship.

We primarily operate across Nakuru, Uasin Gishu, Bungoma, Trans Nzoia, and Nairobi counties — and we're just getting started.

Our Journey

Milestones That Made Us

From a family idea to a growing force in branding across Kenya.

AGI Is Born

Arend Gesin Investment is founded in Nakuru as a family-based branding and imaging business, with a bold vision to serve communities and empower youth.

2020
2021

Expanding Our Services

AGI broadens its offering to include stationery supplies, IT installations, and graphic design — becoming a one-stop branding partner for businesses across the region.
